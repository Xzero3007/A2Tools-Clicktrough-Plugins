# A2Tools Clicktrough · Plugins

The plugins of the **Clicktrough** build of the
[A2Tools DPS Meter](https://github.com/taengu/A2Tools-DPS-Meter) (AION 2):
this repo's own plugins, and the changes to the original's plugins
([taengu/A2Tools-Plugins](https://github.com/taengu/A2Tools-Plugins)) the
build ships. The meter itself, its changes and its releases are in
[A2Tools-DPS-Meter_Clicktrough](https://github.com/Xzero3007/A2Tools-DPS-Meter_Clicktrough).

> [!NOTE]
> A personal project, shared in public because it builds on public, GPL-3.0
> work. Not affiliated with A2 Tools or NCSOFT; no support is promised.

Nothing here is installed on its own: every release of the Clicktrough build
puts these plugins into its package (`build/assemble.sh`), and the meter's
green button brings them. A change here makes a new release at the next
scheduled build (in practice within a few hours), or at once when the
release is started by hand.

## Own plugins (`plugins/<id>`)

| Plugin | What it does |
|---|---|
| Armory | Your character as **NC's AION 2 character pages** show it (read through the meter, patch 0032), every value written out without hovering: the **gear score taken apart** (item levels, enhance, Amplify, Manastones, Theostones, Daevanion points; the game's own formula, exact on 24 characters), the deity stats round a wheel, each piece's stats, stones, extra skill levels and next enhance step (chance, Kinah, materials), the Daevanion boards and titles. **Stats**: what every stat on your character does (Double Chance, Perfect, Endurance, Penetration, the caps), in the game's own words, with where yours come from. **Soul binding**: per piece what it has and the properties you want, picked from what it can get, with the chance a new binding gives them all; the strongest players' picks as suggestions. **Skills**: target levels from a list (yours, the reference's or the strongest players'), each level split into skill points, Daevanion and gear/Arcana, and the way to the target. **Daevanion**: every offensive stat still closed on the boards, a board map, and the cheapest way to each node. **Reference**: questlog.gg build pages saved in the browser, several kept, slot by slot against yours, editable. **Goals**: a target gear score or combat power (an estimate) and the cheapest enhance and Amplify steps to it, with Kinah (average and 9 in 10) and materials, Amplify Stones at your server's market price. **History** of every reading, and the game's own numbers while you play. Enhance tables and items: the game's files, as [Aion2Data](https://aion2data.com) reads them; soul bindings: the game's item data as questlog.gg's item database holds it; the strongest players: Shugo.GG's leaderboard and NC's pages, counted without names. |
| Map | The map you are on (Verteron, Altgard, the Abyss, Poeta, Ishalgen, whole as the game draws its world map; 25 instances) with **your position** (steadied: the meter hears your own place only now and then, so between the fixes the mark moves smoothly and a ring shows how far off it may be), the pets Pet Finder follows (red; favourites ★ with a gold ring, the one being farmed with a white one; Pet Soul Tracker's only without Pet Finder), the pet monsters around you, **Monsters** around you, **Gathering** spots by kind and **Kibelisk** camps, each layer on or off (the gathering kinds by kind: Herbs, Ore, Wood, Fibers, Cooking, Gemstones, Odyle); the next pet to go for, which way and how far (pinned pets first, then farmed, then the ones still needed; or pinned and farmed only, or off). **Hidden Cubes** (the known spots, and one in range), **Feathers** (the Monolith pieces), **Places** (sealed dungeons, battlefields, outposts, villages, region names); the region you are in. **Field bosses** (in Places), marks 100–175 % (125 % at first), and the gathering spots, Hidden Cubes and pet monsters your meter sees added where the map had none and kept (the data knows few: Asvata 3 in Altgard; 0.6.0 fixed that they were never kept, and learns also where the map is only guessed). **Pets: favourites / not at level 3 / all** (pets not at level 3 in their genus colour, as the Pet Soul Tracker 0.5.0's map shows them), and under the map the **nearest monsters with souls you still need** (up to four: level, how many, which way and how far, the pet and its souls left). **Search** for a field boss, a gathering spot (Orichalcum, Aria ...), a pet, a monster, a Kibelisk camp, Hidden Cube (known spots or every place one can appear), Monolith, a place or a region: all its places with a gold ring and the way there: walking, or a teleport to the Kibelisk nearest the place and a line from it, whichever is shorter; Next says which ("teleport to <Kibelisk>, then 186 m"). Map buttons in Event Timers (world bosses) and the Market (gathered materials) do the same, until its × stops it (in the Map window or at the bottom of the overlay). Without the zone (the meter started after you logged in) the map goes by what is around you, else the map you were on last. **Overlay** opens a small map that follows you over the game: sizes S, M, L, XL or any size from its corners (patch 0013), its own opacity, the wheel zooms, lockable so clicks go to the game. The world maps are the game's own pictures as the [AION2 Interactive Map](https://github.com/aion2-interactive-map/aion2-interactive-map) publishes them, calibrated by the game's map bounds (2 % larger than before, up to 90 m better at Altgard's edges; the Kibelisks agree within 6–8 m); data from SkeeveAN's [Aion-DPS-Meter](https://github.com/SkeeveAN/Aion-DPS-Meter) (MIT) and the AION2 Interactive Map (CC BY-NC 4.0, non-commercial). Which monster gives which pet's souls comes from the game's drop tables, as in Pet Finder (0.8.1): before, a second Contaminated Swarm in Altgard showed as a Drana Swarm, whose souls drop only in Verteron. The map you are on follows **what is around you** when the zone the meter heard is clearly out of date (3 or more monsters or spots of another map, more than of the zone's): going over to the Elyos side, the map and the overlay stayed on Altgard (0.8.2). **Where the game puts a monster the data lacks** (0.9.0): from the game's own tables (the Pet Soul Tracker's data), the middle one of its spawn points, so the 39 pet monsters SkeeveAN's data lacks (Harsh Pino Worker, Hill Ursus ...) show and have the way there; shown from a search or a map link while the map knows fewer than the game has, the subzones they lie in are outlined in gold and the bar says how many it knows ("The game has 22 spawn points here in Aullaeu Village; the map knows 1"). **Every pet monster's spawn points** (0.10.0): all of them as the game has them, from A2 Tools' Pet Soul Tracker's map data (Aion 2 Timers'): 458 of 465 pet monsters with exactly the game's count (Harsh Pino Worker 22, Hill Coradon 91), checked in every build; and **Eltnen, Morheim and the Abyss's middle and upper layers** as maps of their own, with their field bosses (the middle and upper layers showed the lower one before). **Everything Aion 2 Timers' map has** (0.11.0, the game client's own data): every Kibelisk, gathering spot (about 2,700 in Verteron, 1,400 in Eltnen), place a Hidden Cube can appear, dungeon entrance, Spacetime Rift, flight circle, passage, **Services** (merchants, storage, crafting tables, teleport towers), **Quests** (quest givers and their quests), every monster's and **NPC**'s spawn points and the areas' names, each a layer, all searchable (a quest by its name too); Eltnen and Morheim at the game's full size (8192 px). Drawn with **the game's own minimap symbols** (the Hidden Cubes keep their cube), the names decluttered; **every mark can be clicked**: that thing everywhere on the map with the way to the nearest, clicked again it's off. "Empyrean Traces" were the Monolith pieces again: out of Gathering, the layer is **Feathers**. The meter also **learns** Kibelisks, feathers and places it sees where the map has none. 0.11.1: **no stripes** on the maps where their tiles meet (Verteron's pink band from SkeeveAN's tiles, the game's own one-pixel edge lines in Altgard, Ishalgen, the Abyss and Poeta), the symbols **40 % larger**, and a **field boss** says it has one place in the game (Bodyguard Teegant, Divine Ansas). 0.11.2: buttons on the map (**+ − Fit Results Big map**), a **scale**, each gathering kind with its **count**, and a map that started in a small box is **fitted again** when it grows. 0.11.3: no **Fibers** chip while the game places none (it comes by itself with the first fiber spot your meter sees). 0.11.4: the **map picker** is its own list (gold, not the desktop's blue) in groups, **In the game**, **Coming later**, **Instances**, **Starting areas**, each but the first hideable (the starting areas hidden at first); a map to come moves to In the game once the meter sees you on it. |
| Market | Your server's market prices ([aion2.exchange](https://aion2.exchange)'s, first from the fork's hourly copy of them on GitHub (branch `market-data`, workflow `market-prices.yml`: aion2.exchange is slow and often briefly down), else from its public API; through patch 0016) of the **gathered materials**: Odyle, ore, gemstones, logs, herbs, cooking ingredients, lowest listing, listings, sold in 28 days and average, change. **Odyle morph**: Substance Morph turns 1 Odyle into 1 gathered material of the same grade; each material's market price against its Odyle and what morphing saves (morphed materials are bound: for crafting only), and what Odyle Energy costs through morph. **Recipes** of your faction by profession: what the product sells for (less the market's fee, 10 % unless changed), the materials bought on the market or with Odyle morphed where cheaper, the profit; a click shows each material with count, price and total, and the combo. **Materials**: every material with its price and the recipes that need it, how many and what that costs. Every list in one column layout, its header kept at the top while scrolling; **Asmodian / Elyos** beside Update (remembered): only that side's materials and recipes; a **star** makes an item a favourite in every tab and **Favorites** lists them first; a click on a gathered material or an Odyle morph row lists the recipes that need it; a **map button** beside a gathered material shows where it is gathered on the Map plugin. Recipes and names from the game client via SkeeveAN's [Aion-DPS-Meter](https://github.com/SkeeveAN/Aion-DPS-Meter) (MIT); NPC prices as MetaBot.GG lists them, changeable. The credit to aion2.exchange is text, not a link (0.3.1). |
| Pet Finder | **One pet list**: the **Pets** tab is the Pet Soul Tracker's list, whole (A2 Tools, GPL-3.0: levels, souls to level 3, where souls drop, Farming, filters, renaming, pop up while farming), with **favourites (☆/★)** that are first in every tab and go to the Map with the pet you are farming; Pet Soul Tracker's pins are taken over once. **Nearby**: pet mobs near you **with how far and which way** and a map button each; **Merchant**: a shopping list for the Black Cloud Merchant. Nearby and Merchant data and packet layouts from [PetTracker](https://github.com/0Fox2Give/PetTracker) (GPLv3). A monster's map link shows its spawn points on the Map instead of a website. **Nearest** sorts the Pets tab by the closest spawn on your map (from the Map). The button right of the tabs puts the **Merchant** tab's three lists side by side as columns, a wide bar; too narrow for that, they wrap under each other. English only, in the meter's neutral colours. Each place a soul drops has a **Map** button that shows the monster's spawn points in the Map plugin; nothing links to a website (0.4.2). Each pet in the Pets tab has **its portrait** beside its name, as in Nearby and Merchant (0.4.3); in the **Cards** layout it sits at the card's left, as tall as the card, and stays at the top left of an open card with where its souls drop beside it (0.4.4). The Pets tab's highlights (chosen filters, their count, the bar of how far along every pet is, the links) are **gold, not blue** (0.4.4). An **open card's portrait grows** to the card's height, sharp (PetTracker's portraits at 256 px, `portraits/`, loaded only for the open card), with the sources beside it (0.4.5). |

They come with every package, with no install and no developer mode. What
lies in a plugin's `dev/` (data generators, tests) stays out of the package;
`dev/ci.sh` checks the plugin on every build. If you loaded such a plugin
earlier through "Load plugin folder": a developer folder wins over the
bundled copy, so remove it there. Settings and saved data stay; they belong
to the id.

## The original's plugins (`original/`)

The build takes the original's plugins frozen at one commit
(`original/PIN`); single plugins can be taken at another
(`original/pins.txt`), for a new plugin or a new version once we decided to
take it. New plugins and new versions of the original are only reported (an
issue in the app repo, label `plugin-watch`), never taken in by themselves.
Each runs from the package only where it is installed (Settings → Plugins →
Available), unless changed here without an `installed-only` file.

| Plugin | What's different |
|---|---|
| Daily Checklist | **Side by side**: the button next to "Edit" puts Daily, Weekly and One-time side by side as columns, a wide bar. Too narrow for that, they wrap under each other. |
| Pet Soul Tracker | **Not shipped and doesn't run** (patch 0026): its list is **Pet Finder's Pets tab**, with the changes for the Map. |
| Roster / Content Overview | At the original's **1.4.4** (2026-10-28: an Elyos character's Odyle Energy crafts counted, they showed 0; no empty copy of the last character when the meter starts before the game). **Values dated as the game said them** (with patch 0028): a count the game reports again unchanged is no longer shown as reset (≈0). A shop or craft row not seen yet says to **open the shop or the crafting window** on that character (the game sends them only then), not to log in. Runs where installed, as before. |
| Enhanced Boss Telegraph | **No link** to the original's issue tracker on GitHub. |
| Event Timers | **Folded**, with the controls off, it shows the **bosses** below the next events too: scheduled bosses running or starting soon, and world bosses that are up, in their window or back soon, in three parts: **now** (up, running), **soon** (in their spawn window, or within 15 min) and **later** (within Settings → "Folded: bosses due within": off, 30 min, 1 h, 2 h, 3 h; 1 h at first). The next **Artifact Siege** always stays visible folded, however far off. The bar at the top of the folded view has a button for each part (**Events, Now, Soon, Later**) to show or hide it, and the layout button (or Settings → "Folded: layout") puts the parts **side by side** as columns, a wide bar; too narrow for that, they wrap under each other. Side by side an empty part keeps its column. A **map button** beside each world boss (on the Bosses tab, and folded after its map's name) shows it on the Map plugin, with the Kibelisk to teleport to and the way there. The credit to Aion 2 Timers under the world bosses is text, not a link. At the original's **0.6.19** (2026-10-28): the scheduled bosses are the **Abyss bosses**, Argo with them, and one up and fought shows its **live HP** from the shared timer file (read only; other players' meters report it); a scheduled boss stays up for 15 minutes. Folded, an Abyss boss fought now shows in Now with its HP. |

The changes are patches, `original/<id>/*.patch`, applied to the plugin at
its pinned commit. In Settings such a plugin is tagged "Clicktrough"; Remove
takes it away until it is installed again under Available.

`original/left-out.txt`: the original's plugins never shipped or run (the
meter's patch 0026), with why.
