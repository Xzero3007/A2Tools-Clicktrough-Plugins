The enhance tables (Enchant, Amplify), what each level adds, the list of
enhanceable items and the stats' descriptions in data.js are AION 2's game
data, read from the game's files by Aion2Data (aion2data.com, an unofficial
fan reference, not affiliated with NCSOFT), which serves them for its enhance
calculator and its stats page.
