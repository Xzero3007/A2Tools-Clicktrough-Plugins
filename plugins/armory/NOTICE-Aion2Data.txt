The enhance tables (Enchant, Amplify), what each level adds, and the list of
enhanceable items in data.js are AION 2's game data, read from the game's
files by Aion2Data (aion2data.com, an unofficial fan reference, not affiliated
with NCSOFT), which serves them as the data file of its enhance calculator.
