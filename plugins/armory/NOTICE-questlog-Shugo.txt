The soul binding lists in data.js (which stats, in which range and by which
weight, and which skill effects each item's soul binding can give, and how
many), the Arcana cards, the grades of Manastones and Theostones and the
names of stats and skills by their ids are AION 2's game data, read from
the game's files; they are taken from questlog.gg's item database (an unofficial fan site, not affiliated with
NCSOFT), which its character builder uses. The share of the stats against
the skill effects (80 to 20) is NCSOFT's probability notice as Aion2Data
reads it.

What the strongest players keep (data.js "top") is counted from the
characters on Shugo.GG's combat power leaderboard (an unofficial fan site,
not affiliated with NCSOFT), as NC's AION 2 character pages show them. Only
the counts are kept: no names, servers or ids.
