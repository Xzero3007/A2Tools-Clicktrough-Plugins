bosses.js is Enhanced Boss Telegraph's data file from A2 Tools' plugins
(taengu/A2Tools-Plugins, plugins/boss-telegraph/bosses.js), GPL-3.0,
built by its author from the game's NpcBehaviorCase, NpcTalk, NpcData,
Skill, SkillAbnormal, Dungeon and L10N tables. Used unchanged: the bosses,
their dungeons and names in ten languages, the lines the game shows for
their casts, skills and buffs, and the hand-checked mechanics, which are
Boss Notes' starter sheets. Boss Notes is GPL-3.0 as well (LICENSE).
