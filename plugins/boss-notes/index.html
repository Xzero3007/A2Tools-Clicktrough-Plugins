      Recording

      Every boss fight is recorded: what the boss casts, the buffs it gets and its HP over time, only the boss's, never a player's. Open one in Fights to turn a moment into a phase or an alert.

      Sheets

      Bosses and their lines come from the game's tables, as A2 Tools' Enhanced Boss Telegraph has them (GPL-3.0); its hand-checked mechanics are the starting sheets. The notes are yours: kept by the meter on this computer, sent nowhere.
