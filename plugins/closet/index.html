What the merchant brings

A combine takes 4 skins of one grade and gives one: the next grade at NCSOFT's published chance (Common 25%, Rare 20%, Epic 15%, Unique 10%; 35/30/25/15% with a Lucky Aura), else a skin of the same grade. Every skin of a grade is equally likely, so a new one comes as often as the share you lack. "New skins per combine" counts the copies it gives too, which go into the next combines. Fees 600 / 1,000 / 6,000 / 30,000 Kinah. Spares are the closet's extra copies (about: the closet counts them, the game decides which ones it takes).

Extract settings (Auto Extract)

Extracting gives the piece's look as a skin: Common, Rare and Epic gear one time in four, Unique one in ten, so a new skin comes as often as that times the share of the grade's looks you lack. A grade whose looks you nearly all have wastes the extraction: sell those pieces (or dismantle them for materials) instead.
