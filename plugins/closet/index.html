Chances and fees are NCSOFT's published ones (Common 25%, Rare 20%, Legend 15%, Unique 10% to the next grade; 35/30/25/15% with a Lucky Aura). Every skin of the grade that comes out is equally likely, so the chance of a new one is the share of that grade you lack. A combine that does not go up is counted as a random skin of the same grade (sources differ on this). The skins you put in are not counted: use your spare copies.

Extracting gear gives its skin too: Common, Rare and Legend gear one time in four, Unique one in ten.

Skins: AION 2's game data via SkeeveAN's Aion-DPS-Meter (MIT) and Aion2Data. Prices: the merchant's own offers.
