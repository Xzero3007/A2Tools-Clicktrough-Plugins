The item names and grades, the gear's and crafting items' icon names and the
content tickets (names, highest counts, resets) in data.js come from
SkeeveAN's Aion-DPS-Meter, https://github.com/SkeeveAN/Aion-DPS-Meter
(Client/assets/aion2/items/item_names.json, items/item_icons.json,
craft/item_icons.json, tickets/tickets.json), read there from the game
client. The meter's patch 0034, which reads your items from the game for
this plugin, follows that project's reading of the inventory and the drops
(Aion2Stock.cs, Aion2Loot.cs). That project's tooling and data are under
