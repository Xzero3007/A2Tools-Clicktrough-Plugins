Where and when

    Gold items

      Statistics

      Where it came from: the zone you are in (open world with its towns, dungeon, Abyss with the Abyss Corridor, the Shugo Festival's games, the Incursion of the Spirits as the Dimensional Invasion), and a Shugo Festival or Dimensional Invasion key used marks the rewards around it as that event's. Bought, crafted and broken-down items are no drop. The open world's Kina is the game's counter toward the daily cap; elsewhere (the Abyss goes on past the cap) the Kina coming in. Worth: the market price when it dropped. A day counts for a source with half an hour or more played there; days start counting when this version runs.

      Shown in the floater: tick what you want to see.

      Limits

        Keys and entry counts

          Counts come from the game when you log in and each time one changes. The plugin keeps each character's last ones: a dimmed value is from an earlier login (hover it for when), shown until the game sends it again, as after the meter started mid-game.

          Items and tickets: AION 2's game data via SkeeveAN's Aion-DPS-Meter (MIT) and Aion2Data. Prices: aion2.exchange (players' collected market prices).
