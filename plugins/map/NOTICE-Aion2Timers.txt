From Aion 2 Timers' interactive map (aion2timers.com, its map data:
map/data/maps.json, positions/<map>.json, tiles/ and icons/, build in
dev/provenance.json, downloaded by dev/fetch_aion2timers.py):

- in data.js: the Kibelisks, the field bosses, the gathering spots, the
  places a Hidden Cube can appear, the places (dungeon entrances, outposts,
  Spacetime Rifts, flight circles, Monoliths, passages, field events, the
  Abyss's artifacts and corridors), the services (merchants, storage,
  crafting tables, teleport towers), the quest givers and their quests, the
  monsters' and NPCs' spawn points and levels, the areas' names, and the
  object ids the meter learns them by (kibelisks, bosses, gather, cubes,
  places, monsters, npcs, monsterLevels, labels, objectKinds);
- the spawn points of the pet monsters (spawns), also as A2 Tools' Pet Soul
  Tracker has them (plugins/pet-souls/maps.js in
  https://github.com/taengu/A2Tools-Plugins, revision in dev/provenance.json;
  built there by tools/pet-maps/build.py);
- the map pictures of Eltnen and Morheim (tiles/eltnen, tiles/morheim: its
  tiles at the highest zoom, stitched) and of the Abyss's middle and upper
  layers (tiles/abyss_middle, tiles/abyss_upper);
- the symbol of the passages and teleport towers (icons/door.webp,
  icons/npcteleport.webp), the game's.

The spawn points are the game's own data, as the client has it; the pictures
are the game's world maps. AION 2 names, maps and game material belong to their
respective owners (NCSOFT / NC Corporation). Attribution is not a licence to
that material.
