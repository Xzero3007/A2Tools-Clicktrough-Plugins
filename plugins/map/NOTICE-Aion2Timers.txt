The spawn points of the pet monsters in data.js (those of Verteron, Altgard and
the Abyss's lower layer) and the map pictures of Eltnen, Morheim and the
Abyss's middle and upper layers (tiles/eltnen, tiles/morheim,
tiles/abyss_middle, tiles/abyss_upper) come from Aion 2 Timers' interactive
map (aion2timers.com), as A2 Tools' Pet Soul Tracker has them
(plugins/pet-souls/maps.js and maps/ in https://github.com/taengu/A2Tools-Plugins,
revision in dev/provenance.json; built there by tools/pet-maps/build.py).

The spawn points are the game's own data, as the client has it; the pictures
are the game's world maps. AION 2 names, maps and game material belong to their
respective owners (NCSOFT / NC Corporation). Attribution is not a licence to
that material.
