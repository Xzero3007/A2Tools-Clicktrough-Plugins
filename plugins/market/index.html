Substance Morph turns one Odyle into one gathered material of the same grade (Odyle → base, Fine → Fine / Fresh, Pure → Pure, Radiant → Radiant), always. What it makes is bound: it goes into your own crafting, not onto the market. Click a material for the recipes that need it.

One craft that succeeds: failures and combos are not counted. Market buys every material; Morph takes the Odyle instead where that is cheaper. The product sells at its lowest listing, less the fee.

NPC prices of the bound tools

What the NPC shops ask (as MetaBot.GG lists it from the game files); change one if the shop asks otherwise.

Lowest listings far from the item's usual price: the median of its lowest listing over the last days (the hourly copy keeps 30 days and the last 48 hours), until there are enough days the last hours, then the average sale, then the lowest listing an hour before. Far above: ×5 or more; far below: 40 % or less. A click shows the course.

Prices: aion2.exchange (players' collected market prices) · recipes, names and pictures: the game client, via SkeeveAN's Aion-DPS-Meter and Aion2Data
