The Pets tab (list.js, pets.js, sources.js and the #petsView styles in
style.css) is the Pet Soul Tracker plugin by A2 Tools, from
https://github.com/taengu/A2Tools-Plugins (plugins/pet-souls, version 0.4.9),
with the Clicktrough fork's patches (clicktrough/plugins/pet-souls) and the
changes that make it a tab of Pet Finder (marked "Clicktrough" in list.js).
