The icon names of the items SkeeveAN's lists have none for (materials,
consumables, keys ...) and the names of a few items its list lacks are
AION 2's game data, read from the game's files by Aion2Data (aion2data.com,
an unofficial fan reference, not affiliated with NCSOFT), as its item lists
show them (dev/fetch_icons.py). The pictures themselves come from the game's
icon server (assets.playnccdn.com), through the meter.
